Methodology

1

Collect — destination, days, budget, travelers and interests.

2

Validate — check required fields, ranges and input consistency.

3

Orchestrate — convert the goal into planning tasks and tool calls.

4

Retrieve — pull place, route and weather signals where available.

5

Generate — produce a structured candidate itinerary.

6

Verify — independently check cost, duration and travel constraints.

7

Re-plan — replace or reorder activities when a constraint fails, then re-check.
